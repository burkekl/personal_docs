Todd Burkett, 58, passed away on March 6, 2025.

Todd was born on July 26, 1967, to Eugene and Nancy Burkett, both of whom preceded him in death. He grew up with a strong sense of independence and curiosity that carried through every stage of his life.

Todd dedicated many years of service to the Butler Township Police Department in Dayton, Ohio, where he worked both on patrol and as a detective. He took pride in his work and the role he played in protecting and serving his community. Later in life, he moved to Angel Fire, New Mexico, where he worked as a ranger at Camp Barker, a Girl Scouts summer camp — a role that allowed him to be where he was happiest: outdoors.

Todd had a deep love for the outdoors. Hunting, fishing, trapping, archery, and shooting weren’t just hobbies — they were part of who he was. He felt most at home outside, where life was simple and honest, and where he could spend his time doing what he loved.

He also had a lifelong appreciation for cars, firearms, military history, action movies, Monty Python, and 80s rock. And in a way that only makes sense to the people who knew him best, he never lost his fondness for Flying Pizza.

Todd is survived by his wife, Terri Burkett; his children, Max Burkett and Miana Burkett; and his stepchildren, Matt Wiesner, Moriah Wiesner Chapman, and Micah Wiesner. He is also survived by his siblings, Cheryl Burkett Bigony and Kevin Burkett.

He will be remembered for his love of the outdoors, his strong personality, and the life he lived on his own terms. He leaves behind family and friends who will miss him deeply.

A memorial service will be held on Thursday, June 4, 2025, from 5:00 to 7:30 PM at the Vandalia Recreation Center.
